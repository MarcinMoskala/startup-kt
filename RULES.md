# Startup.kt: rules (v3)

*Build a Kotlin startup. Ship the best app. Collect the most ⭐ GitHub Stars.*
2–5 players · 20 minutes · ages 10+

## Setup
Each player draws a **Startup** card. It shows the resource it produces, your starting 💰, and its ability.

## Resources
- 📱 **Frontend** and 🖥 **Backend** come from people (👥 Team cards).
- 🤖 **Agent** cards fill 🤖 slots for free. A 🤖 can also do 📱 or 🖥 work: **pay 1💰 in tokens** each time.
- **Resources are never spent.** Your team works on every build.
- Missing 📱 or 🖥? **Hire a freelancer for 2💰.** You can't hire a 🤖.

## Playing
The game has **3 Sprints**. In each Sprint everyone gets **5 cards**. Then:
1. Everyone picks one card at the same time and reveals it.
2. You either **Build** it (pay its cost and place it in front of you) or **Sell** it (+3💰).
3. Pass the rest of your hand to the player on your **right**.
4. After **4 picks**, discard the last card.

**Libraries 📚** score 2⭐ and make the two features printed on them **free for you**.
You can't build two copies of the same card.

## Scoring
⭐ from your cards and bonuses, plus **1⭐ per 3💰**. On a tie, more 💰 wins.

> 💡 *Tip for your first game: in Sprint I build your team. The ⭐ come later.*

## Two players: the Duel
Use the 4+ cards and deal **8 cards** each. Every pick you play one card **and discard another face down**, then pass.

## Card types
| Icon | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 👥 | Team | Produces 📱 / 🖥 / 🤖 for the rest of the game |
| ⭐ | Feature | Costs resources, scores ⭐ |
| 📚 | Library | Scores 2⭐ and makes two features free |
| 💼 | Business | 💰 now, or ⭐ at the end for what you built |
